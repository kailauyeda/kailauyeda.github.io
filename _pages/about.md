I am an Earth System Science PhD candidate in the [Ocean and Climate Dynamics Group](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/drakelab/) at UC Irvine, where I am advised by Dr. Henri Drake. I spend my summers at Los Alamos National Laboratory as a Center for Space and Earth Science Fellow. There, I am a member of the Fluid Dynamics and Solid Mechanics Group, advised by Drs. [Katherine Smith](https://www.linkedin.com/in/katherine-smith-6155bbb6/) and [Dylan Schlichting](https://dylanschlichting.github.io/). I am interested in studying how the formation and transformation of Antarctic Bottom Water changes over time. To do so, I use the [Energy Exascale Earth System Model](e3sm.org).

I graduated from Haverford College with a B.S. in Mathematics and a minor in Health Studies. I then worked as a Research Assistant in the Texas A&M Department of Oceanography, advised by [Dr. Spencer Jones](https://opatt-group.github.io/). My research at Texas A&M studied how background diffusivity parameterizations impact heat transport in the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation. I used MOM6 to run experiments in an idealized model with a Lagrangian particle tracking package.

Research Interests
======
1. Bottom Water and Deep Water circulation.
2. Southern Ocean dynamics and dense shelf overflows.
3. Quantification of physical and numerical mixing.
4. Water Mass Transformation framework.
